Pagenest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21. Publish this text (see docs/web/privacy-policy.html) at the URL configured in AppConfig.PRIVACYPOLICYURL before the Play listing goes live.

Pagenest (“the app”, package com.xprin.pagenest) is a document and ebook reader published by XPrin (“we”). Contact: xprin1company@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • Your documents, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading progress and statistics stay on your device. We have no servers and no user accounts, and the app never uploads what you read.
  • The app works fully offline (reading, notes, search, dictionary).
  • The internet is used only for advertising (Google AdMob) and anonymous usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics, which you can switch off), and when you tap a web link.

2. What is stored on your device

Copies of documents you import (private app storage), a local database with your highlights/notes/bookmarks/collections/progress/reading time, settings, thumbnails and converted pages (cache), and the offline dictionary. You can delete a document (it moves to *Recently deleted*, then is removed), clear caches, or delete everything by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling. Uninstalling or clearing data permanently deletes this data unless you made a backup (*Settings → Backup & restore* creates a file you save wherever you choose).

3. Permissions

INTERNET, ACCESSNETWORKSTATE (ads/analytics, connectivity checks by the Ads SDK), AD_ID (Advertising ID for ads, only if you consent where required). Pagenest does not request access to all files, contacts, location, camera or microphone. Files are added only through the system file picker, “open with/share to” from other apps, or folders you choose.

4. Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob in the library, collections and settings screens only — never while you read. AdMob may collect and use the Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires, we ask for your consent with Google’s consent form and you can change your choice at any time in *Settings → Privacy & ads*. See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Analytics

If enabled, Firebase Analytics receives anonymous counts of feature use (for example “a PDF was opened”, “a word lookup happened”). It never receives document content, file names, paths, notes, highlighted text or the words you search for. Firebase also collects standard technical data (app instance identifier, device model, OS version, country). Turn it off in *Settings → Privacy & ads*. See: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with Google (AdMob/Firebase) as described above. When *you* share a document, page or text, it goes to the app you choose through Android’s share sheet using a temporary link; Pagenest never creates public links.

7. Optional features that touch the network

None required. If a future version adds an online feature (for example an AI explainer), it will be opt-in, clearly labelled, and will not send document text without your explicit consent.

8. Children

Pagenest is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Your choices & rights

Disable analytics; change ad consent; delete data on the device; request information or deletion of any data held about you by emailing us (we hold none beyond what Google processes as described; for Google-held data use Google’s account tools).

10. Changes

We will update this page and the “last updated” date when this policy changes.
